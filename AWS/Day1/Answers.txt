2)

For accessing resources from another account, we can use the assumerole in trust policy

In account B,
    we can assumerole for that a particular role in account A, so that in account B , 
    it can access resources with given restriction

    then allow only the required resources in the account b for that role created in account B

In account A,
    add a policy to the role, to act as other role in account B, using assumerole

In this way, we can access one account's resources from other account.
